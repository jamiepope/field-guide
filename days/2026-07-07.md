## Today's Goal

Keep the day loose and Left Bank-focused, with one fixed point: the Catacombs at 6:15 PM.

<div class="today-callout">

## Before Leaving the Hotel

- Catacombs ticket downloaded and accessible offline.
- Comfortable shoes.
- Light layer for underground.
- No large bags.
- Water before the Catacombs, not inside.
- Phone charged.

## Evening

### Catacombs of Paris

<div class="field-stop">

**5 min walk** • arrive by about 5:55 PM

Walk from Rue Daguerre to Place Denfert-Rochereau. Use the official entrance at **1 Avenue du Colonel Henri Rol-Tanguy** and keep the ticket ready.

</div>

<div class="field-nav">

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Rue+Daguerre+Paris&destination=Catacombs+of+Paris+1+Avenue+du+Colonel+Henri+Rol-Tanguy+Paris&travelmode=walking)
•
[Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/?saddr=Rue%20Daguerre%20Paris&daddr=Catacombs%20of%20Paris%201%20Avenue%20du%20Colonel%20Henri%20Rol-Tanguy%20Paris&dirflg=w)
•
[Official Site](https://www.catacombes.paris.fr/en)
•
[Tickets](https://www.billetterie-parismusees.paris.fr/)

</div>

<div class="field-facts">

<div>
<dt>Entry</dt>
<dd><strong>6:15 PM</strong></dd>
</div>

<div>
<dt>Visit</dt>
<dd>About 45 minutes on a one-way route.</dd>
</div>

<div>
<dt>Bags</dt>
<dd>No suitcases or large bags. Keep bags under 40 × 30 × 20 cm.</dd>
</div>

<div>
<dt>Stairs</dt>
<dd>131 down, 112 up.</dd>
</div>

<div>
<dt>Inside</dt>
<dd>Cool, humid, dim, uneven, and about 14°C.</dd>
</div>

</div>

<strong>Notes</strong>

<p>No eating or drinking inside. Photos are okay without flash or tripod. Do not touch the bones.</p>
