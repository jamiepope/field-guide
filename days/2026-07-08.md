## Today's Goal

Make the conference day easy: arrive fed, know where you are going, keep notes, and leave the evening flexible.

<div class="today-callout">
  <span>The conference is the anchor. Everything else should support a good day at the venue: coffee, badge, lunch backup, phone battery, and a simple dinner plan.</span>
</div>

---

## Before Leaving the Hotel

- Conference badge or registration confirmation.
- Laptop, charger, and power bank.
- Phone fully charged.
- Notebook or notes app ready.
- Water.
- Comfortable shoes.
