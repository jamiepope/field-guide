## Today's Goal

Move hotels without creating a hard day, then enjoy the Seine dinner cruise in the evening.

<div class="today-callout">
  <span>This is a logistics day. The win is a clean checkout, luggage handled, phones charged, and no unnecessary sightseeing before the cruise.</span>
</div>

---

## Before Leaving the Hotel

- Pack completely before leaving the room.
- Check drawers, bathroom, safe, chargers, and passports.
- Dinner cruise confirmation downloaded and available offline.
- Confirm Hôtel Rochechouart check-in time if needed.
- Plan to leave luggage with reception if the room is not yet ready.
- Confirm cruise provider, pier, boarding time, and dress guidance.
- Charge phones and power bank.
