## Why It Matters

Transportation should require as little thought as possible. The goal is to decide once, before the trip, and then simply tap and go for the rest of the week.

---

## Official Facts

### Supported Devices

- iPhone supports Navigo cards in Apple Wallet.
- Android devices support Navigo through the Île-de-France Mobilités app (NFC compatible devices).

### Physical Cards

A physical Navigo card is **not required** if using a compatible smartphone.

### Metro / Train / RER

The Metro-Train-RER ticket is valid on:

- Metro
- RER
- Transilien trains

This includes travel to **Versailles Château–Rive Gauche**.

### Weekly Pass

The Navigo Week runs **Monday through Sunday** regardless of the purchase date.
