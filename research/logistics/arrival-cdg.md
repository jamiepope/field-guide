# Arrival at Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)

## Objective

Get from the aircraft to Novotel Paris Les Halles with the least stress possible.

---

# Official Facts

## Immigration

Follow the **Arrivées / Baggage Claim** signs.

Complete immigration before collecting checked luggage.

---

## Customs

After collecting luggage, proceed through Customs.

Nothing special is required unless declaring goods.

---

## Taxi

After exiting Customs:

- Follow the official **Taxi** signs.
- Ignore anyone inside the terminal offering a taxi.
- Continue to the official taxi queue outside your terminal.

Official taxi queues are staffed by airport personnel.

---

## Official Taxi Fare

CDG uses fixed taxi fares to destinations within Paris.

Current official fares:

- Right Bank: **€56**
- Left Bank: **€65**

Novotel Paris Les Halles is on the **Right Bank**, so the expected fare is **€56**.

Traffic does **not** change the fixed fare.

---

## Payment

Official taxis accept credit cards.

Confirm with the driver before departure if paying by card.

---

## Taxi Company

No reservation is necessary.

Simply use the official airport taxi queue.

Many taxis will be G7, Paris' largest taxi company.

---

# Our Plan

- Take an official airport taxi.
- Do **not** use the RER on arrival.
- Do **not** purchase transit passes today.
- Go directly to the hotel.
- Drop luggage.
- Begin exploring on foot.

---

# Recommendations

- Keep the hotel address available on your phone.
- Have passports readily accessible until checked into the hotel.
- Ignore anyone approaching inside the terminal offering transportation.
- Use the staffed taxi queue only.

---

# Open Questions

- Recommended grocery near hotel.
- Nearby pharmacy.
- Best place to buy water after arrival.
