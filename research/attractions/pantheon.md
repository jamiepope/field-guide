# Pantheon

## Why It Matters

The Panthéon is the final resting place of many of France's most influential figures, including Victor Hugo, Voltaire, Rousseau, Marie Curie, Émile Zola, and Alexandre Dumas. It combines impressive architecture with French history and is one of the defining landmarks of the Latin Quarter.

---

## Official Facts

### Opening Hours

- 1 April – 30 September: **10:00 AM – 6:30 PM**
- Last admission: **45 minutes before closing.**
- The monument may stop admitting visitors earlier during periods of very high attendance.

### Special Hours

**Important for our trip**

The **first working Monday of each month opens at 12:00 PM.**

Since we are visiting on **Monday, July 6**, we should **not** plan to arrive before noon.

### Closures

Annual closures:

- January 1
- May 1
- December 25

### Tickets

- Admission includes both the **nave** and the **crypt**.
- Tickets may be purchased online or at the monument.

### Self-Guided Visit

- Self-guided visit documents are available free of charge.
- Guides are available in multiple languages.

### Guided Tours

- Free guided tours are sometimes offered during the afternoon, subject to availability.
- No advance reservation is required.

### Bags

- There is **no luggage storage**.
- Bags larger than **20 × 40 × 40 cm** are not permitted.

## Trip Decision

- Visit immediately after lunch.
- Plan approximately **60–90 minutes**.
- Visit both the nave and the crypt.
- Do not attempt to visit before noon because of the special Monday opening.
