# Notre-Dame Cathedral

## Why It Matters

Notre-Dame de Paris is one of the world's most important Gothic cathedrals and has stood at the heart of Paris for more than 850 years. Following the 2019 fire and years of restoration, this will be one of the first opportunities to experience the cathedral after its reopening.

---

## Official Facts

### Entry

- Entry to the cathedral is free.
- No ticket is required.

### Reservations

- Free reservations are optional.
- Reservations are intended only to reduce waiting times.
- New reservation slots are released only a short time before the visit.
- Reservations cannot be made weeks or months in advance.
- Walk-up visitors are always welcome.

### Opening Hours

- Monday–Friday: 7:50 AM – 7:00 PM
- Saturday–Sunday: 8:15 AM – 7:30 PM
- Last entry: 30 minutes before closing.

### Dress Code

As an active place of worship, visitors should wear modest clothing.

The official guidance requests:

- Cover shoulders.
- Cover torso, stomach, and thighs.
- Avoid transparent or tight-fitting clothing.
- Men should remove hats indoors.

### Inside the Cathedral

- Silence is requested.
- Phone calls are prohibited.
- Photography is permitted provided it does not disturb worship or visitors.

### Security

Visitors pass through security screening before entering.

---

## Trip Decision

- Visit Monday morning.
- Arrive early to minimize waiting.
- Check for a free reservation 1–2 days before, but do not depend on obtaining one.
- Plan approximately 60–90 minutes.
