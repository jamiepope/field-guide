# Luxembourg Gardens

## Why It Matters

The Luxembourg Gardens are one of Paris's most beautiful public parks and provide a relaxed break between major historic attractions. Rather than rushing from monument to monument, this is a place to slow down, sit, and enjoy the atmosphere.

---

## Official Facts

### Admission

- Entry is free.
- No reservation is required.

### Opening Hours

The gardens open and close at different times throughout the year.

During July, the gardens are generally open from **7:30 AM until approximately 9:30 PM**.

### Facilities

- Public restrooms are available.
- Drinking fountains are located throughout the gardens.
- Numerous benches and chairs are available for visitors.

### Accessibility

The main paths are accessible, although some secondary paths are gravel.

---

## Trip Decision

- Visit after lunch.
- No fixed schedule.
- Plan to spend 45–90 minutes simply enjoying the gardens.
- This is intentionally "unscheduled" time.
