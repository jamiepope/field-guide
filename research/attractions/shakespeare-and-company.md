# Shakespeare and Company

## Why It Matters

Shakespeare and Company is one of the world's most famous independent bookstores and has been a gathering place for writers, readers, and travelers for decades. Its location directly across from Notre-Dame makes it a natural stop after visiting the cathedral.

---

## Official Facts

### Opening Hours

- Monday–Wednesday: 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- Thursday–Sunday: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM

### Nearby

A café is located adjacent to the bookstore, making it a convenient stop before continuing into the Latin Quarter.

Research the café separately if we decide to include it in the guide.

### Events

- The bookstore regularly hosts author talks, readings, and literary events.
- Check the official calendar if interested.

### Accessibility

- The bookstore is small and can become crowded.
- Expect narrow aisles and stairs between rooms.

---

## Trip Decision

- Visit immediately after Notre-Dame.
- Spend 30–45 minutes browsing.
- Have coffee here only if seating is available; otherwise continue into the Latin Quarter.
