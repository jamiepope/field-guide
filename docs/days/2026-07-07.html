Today's Goal

Keep the day loose and Left Bank-focused, with one fixed point: the Catacombs at 6:15 PM.

Today is about pacing. Start with coffee, pick up an easy lunch, wander south at your own speed, and arrive at the Catacombs calm and unburdened.

Before Leaving the Hotel

  • Catacombs ticket downloaded and accessible offline.
  • Comfortable shoes.
  • Light layer for underground.
  • No large bags.
  • Water before the Catacombs, not inside.
  • Phone charged.

Evening

Catacombs of Paris

5 min walk • arrive by about 5:55 PM

Walk from Rue Daguerre to Place Denfert-Rochereau. Use the official entrance at 1 Avenue du Colonel Henri Rol-Tanguy and keep the ticket ready.

Google Maps • Apple Maps • Official Site • Tickets

Entry
6:15 PM
Visit
About 45 minutes on a one-way route.
Bags
No suitcases or large bags. Keep bags under 40 × 30 × 20 cm.
Stairs
131 down, 112 up.
Inside
Cool, humid, dim, uneven, and about 14°C.

Notes

No eating or drinking inside. Photos are okay without flash or tripod. Do not touch the bones.
