Today's Goal

Enjoy the Musée d'Orsay without trying to see everything, then let the afternoon become a relaxed westward walk through Paris icons.

The museum is the anchor. The Arc de Triomphe and Eiffel Tower are exterior views today, not more ticketed attractions. Stop before the day turns into a forced march.

Before Leaving the Hotel

  • Musée d'Orsay ticket downloaded and accessible offline.
  • Comfortable walking shoes.
  • Water.
  • Phone fully charged.
  • Light bag only.
  • From Hôtel Rochechouart, Line 12 from Pigalle runs straight down to the Left Bank and the museum.
