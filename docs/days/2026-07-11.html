Saturday, July 11

Theme

Art, Grand Boulevards & Paris Icons

Anchor

Musée d'Orsay

Today's Goal

Experience some of Paris's greatest art before spending the afternoon and evening enjoying its grand boulevards and iconic landmarks.

Before Leaving the Hotel

  • Musée d'Orsay ticket
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Water bottle
  • Phone fully charged

Morning

Visit the Musée d'Orsay.

Take your time.

Focus on the Impressionist collection rather than trying to see everything.

Lunch

Lunch nearby after leaving the museum.

Afternoon

Walk toward Place de la Concorde.

Continue along the Champs-Élysées.

Visit the Arc de Triomphe.

Do not go to the top.

Continue toward the Eiffel Tower if energy and time allow.

Evening

See the Eiffel Tower.

Enjoy dinner nearby.

Take your time walking back or use the Metro if tired.

Transportation

Walking is preferred.

Metro if it meaningfully reduces walking late in the day.
