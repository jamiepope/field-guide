Monday, July 6

Theme

Historic Paris

Anchor

Notre-Dame and the Latin Quarter

Today's Goal

Experience the heart of historic Paris without rushing. Stay almost entirely on foot and leave time to wander.

Before Leaving the Hotel

  • Wear comfortable walking shoes.
  • Dress appropriately for entering churches.
  • Check whether a free Notre-Dame reservation is available.
  • Carry water.

Morning

Walk from Novotel Paris Les Halles to Notre-Dame Cathedral.

Spend time exploring the cathedral before crossing to the Left Bank.

Continue to Shakespeare and Company.

Take time to browse.

Late Morning

Wander through the Latin Quarter.

Enjoy coffee or simply explore the neighborhood.

Do not rush to the Panthéon.

Remember that it opens at 12:00 PM today.

Afternoon

Visit the Panthéon.

Take time to explore both the nave and the crypt.

Afterward, walk to the Luxembourg Gardens.

Relax.

This is intentionally unstructured time.

Evening

Walk back toward Les Halles.

Dinner near the hotel or somewhere discovered during the walk.

Transportation

Primary transportation today: Walking

Metro only if tired on the return journey.
