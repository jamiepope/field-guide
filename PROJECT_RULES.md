# Paris Field Guide - Project Rules

## Source of Truth

MASTER_ITINERARY.md is the authoritative trip plan.

Every day in `days/` must reflect the Master Itinerary.

The website is a rendering of those files.

---

## Workflow

1. Plan in MASTER_ITINERARY.md
2. Approve
3. Update the corresponding day
4. Build
5. Review
6. Commit

---

## Travel Philosophy

- Minimize unnecessary backtracking.
- Build days around neighborhoods.
- One primary anchor per day.
- Timed tickets drive the schedule.
- Prefer walking.
- Keep lunch simple unless intentionally planned.
- Stay flexible around coffee and pastries.
- Dinner should generally finish near the final destination of the day.

---

## Rendering Rules

- Every place becomes a clickable destination.
- Include Apple Maps.
- Include Google Maps.
- Include official website when applicable.
- Walking directions should be practical and concise.

---

## Design Rules

- Monday (July 6) is the visual template.
- Keep cards consistent.
- Highlight the day's primary anchor.
- Mobile-first.
- Avoid unnecessary scrolling.
