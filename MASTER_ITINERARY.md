# Tuesday · July 7

## Theme

The Left Bank

## Anchor

Catacombs — 6:15 PM timed entry

## Goal

Enjoy Saint-Germain and the Left Bank at a relaxed pace, gradually working south toward the Catacombs.

## Morning

### Coffee

Primary:
- Café de Flore

Backups:
- Les Deux Magots
- Café Louise

After coffee:

- Wander Saint-Germain-des-Prés
- Rue de Buci
- Rue Jacob
- Cour du Commerce Saint-André
- Browse small shops and side streets
- No schedule

## Lunch

Buy a takeaway "formule."

Primary:
- Maison Maertens

Backups:
- Eric Kayser
- La Grande Épicerie

Eat lunch in Jardin Catherine-Labouré.

## Afternoon

Walk south toward Denfert-Rochereau.

Spend time on Rue Daguerre.

Browse:

- Bakeries
- Cheese shops
- Produce markets
- Chocolatiers

Optional coffee if anyone wants a break.

## Evening

### Catacombs

Arrive by 5:50 PM.

6:15 PM entry.

Allow about one hour.

### Dinner

Primary:
- Le Cornichon

Backups:
- La Chope Daguerre
- L'Assiette

Stay in the neighborhood after dinner before returning to the hotel.

## Why This Works

One continuous walk through the Left Bank ending naturally at the Catacombs, with no unnecessary backtracking.
